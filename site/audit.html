Audit Trail

This page collects the repository notes that define the manuscript's claim boundary, verification scope, and external-review handoff. The paper is a density-and-reduction manuscript for FMF Collatz growth mechanisms. It is not a proof of the Collatz conjecture.

Completion status: the remaining mathematical gap is the pointwise bridge from density-zero obstruction classes to universal FMF descent. In the current framework this is expressed as an unweighted terminal-window/rank problem.

Current Proof Target

A Collatz proof through this approach would require a pointwise theorem strong enough to show that every odd integer greater than one eventually reaches a smaller odd integer under FMF iteration. The most concrete current sufficient subtarget is a pair of uniform unweighted terminal-window bounds: 

W_R(B,L) <= C_R(B+1)(2/15)^L
W_G(B,L) <= C_G B 112^(-L)

The current manuscript proves weighted, shell, cutoff, and obstruction-class estimates toward these statements, but not the full unweighted bounds.
