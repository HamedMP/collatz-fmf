First-Multiple-of-Four Coordinates for Collatz Growth Chains: Density Bounds and Pointwise Obstruction Reductions

Hamed Mohammadpour

Abstract

We introduce the First-Multiple-of-Four (FMF) decomposition, which partitions Collatz trajectories into odd-to-odd hops through the first value divisible by four. The decomposition yields exact algebraic formulas for hop length and output value, natural-density output laws from 2-adic inverse structure, and an exact asymptotic fractional-moment contraction \(\mathbb{E}[R^{0.53}] \approx 0.8638 < 1\) for the FMF hop ratio.

The main density result is the Quartering Law: conditioned on a growth-B hop, each further growth-B continuation has density factor \(1/4\), so strict growth-B chains of length at least \(k\) have odd-start density \(4^{-k}\). The paper also reduces the first bounded-depth pointwise obstructions to binary return-code and reduced \(\{0,1\}\) gap partial maps, leaving a precise unweighted terminal-window counting problem. These results do not prove the Collatz conjecture.

This is a density-and-reduction manuscript, not a claimed proof of the Collatz conjecture.
